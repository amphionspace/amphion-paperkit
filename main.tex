\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/architecture.pdf}
  \caption{AmphionASR architecture. The audio branch (left) maps a
    16\,kHz waveform through a Whisper-style log-mel front-end, a
    convolutional sub-sampler, the Qwen3-AuT 24-layer Transformer
    encoder, and a Linear--SwooshR--Linear projector to the LLM's
    2560-dimensional hidden space. The text branch (right) tokenizes
    the chat template and replaces four special boundary tokens with
    learnable prompt vectors. The two streams meet at a merge step that
    substitutes the \texttt{<speech>} placeholder with the audio
    embedding sequence, after which Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 performs
    next-token prediction on the assistant span only. See
    Section~\ref{sec:model} for component-level details.}
  \label{fig:teaser}
\end{figure}
