\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{subsec:setup}

\paragraph{Decoding.}
Unless otherwise stated we report greedy-decoded results obtained
through the released vLLM server (\texttt{serve\_vllm.sh}).
% TODO: confirm whether the released numbers in Tables~\ref{tab:asr_en}
% and \ref{tab:asr_zh} are greedy or beam-decoded.

\paragraph{Normalization.}
For English benchmarks we apply the Whisper English text normalizer
\citep{radford2023whisper} to both reference and hypothesis before
computing WER. For Mandarin we apply a stripped-down normalizer that
removes punctuation, normalises full-width / half-width characters and
maps Arabic numerals to their Mandarin equivalents before CER.
For TALCS we apply the union of the two normalizers and report MER.
% TODO: link to the exact normalizer scripts in the released repo.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare against Whisper-large-v3 \citep{radford2023whisper}, the
open-source Qwen3-ASR-1.7B \citep{qwen2025qwen3asr}, and any prior
in-house checkpoint where available.
