\section{Training}
\label{sec:training}

\subsection{Stages}

The released checkpoint
\texttt{qwen3asr\_aut\_qwen3\_4b\_continue\_sft\_ts\_hw\_v3} is the v3
output of a three-stage recipe. Each stage progressively unlocks more
of the model and adds more demanding tasks:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Stage~1 -- Audio--Text Alignment (\texttt{aut}).}
        Encoder and LLM are both frozen; only the adapter is trained on
        plain (audio, transcript) pairs. The objective is to bring
        speech representations into the LLM's token space without yet
        perturbing the LLM's text capabilities.
  \item \textbf{Stage~2 -- Continue SFT (\texttt{continue\_sft}).}
        Adapter and LLM are jointly fine-tuned on a balanced mixture of
        Mandarin and English transcription. The encoder remains frozen.
        We use ms-swift \citep{msswift} for distributed SFT.
  \item \textbf{Stage~3 -- Capability SFT
        (\texttt{ts\_hw\_v3}).} On top of the Stage~2 checkpoint, we
        continue SFT with hotword and target-speaker examples mixed into
        the transcription stream. The mixing ratio is the main knob in
        this stage.

\end{enumerate}

The version suffix \texttt{v3} indicates the third released iteration
of Stage~3; v1 and v2 are described in Section~\ref{sec:analysis} where

\subsection{Objective}

For all three stages we use token-level cross-entropy on the transcript
tokens, with loss masked over the prompt tokens (system instructions,
hotword list, target-speaker reference, audio token positions). For an
input prompt $\mathbf{p}$ and reference transcript
$\mathbf{y} = (y_1, \ldots, y_T)$, the per-example loss is

\[
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = -\sum_{t=1}^{T} \log p_\theta(y_t \mid y_{<t}, \mathbf{p}).
\]
