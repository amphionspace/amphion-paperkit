Speech emotion understanding requires models to recognize not only what emotion category is present, but also how that emotion is expressed through vocal delivery, prosody, and subtle paralinguistic cues. We present \textbf{Amphion EmoAnalyzer}, a compact 1.5B-parameter bilingual speech emotion understanding model that jointly supports speech emotion recognition (SER) and descriptive emotion analysis (DEA). EmoAnalyzer combines multi-task speech-language training with a curated 1,000-hour English-Chinese emotion corpus, enabling the model to ground semantic content through ASR while generating natural-language descriptions of affective state and vocal expression. To improve descriptive fidelity on difficult cases such as sarcasm, tonal shifts, and acoustic degradation, we introduce Emotional Direct Preference Optimization (Emotional DPO), a DEA-focused alignment strategy based on human-adjudicated preferences. Across five open-source SER benchmarks, EmoAnalyzer achieves the strongest average performance in our unified evaluation setting, outperforming the leading SLM baseline in UA (45.49\% vs.\ 37.05\%). On a 1,000-sample human-verified bilingual DEA benchmark, EmoAnalyzer also surpasses proprietary MLLMs including Gemini-3.0-Flash, demonstrating the value of specialized emotion-aware speech-language modeling.
