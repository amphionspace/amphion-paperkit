We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, an open Mandarin and English
SpeechLLM purpose-built for the failure modes that dominate
real-world deployments rather than open-benchmark leaderboards: rare
named entities, overlapping speakers, and noisy or far-field
acoustic conditions. The model couples a Whisper-style audio encoder
with Qwen3-4B-Instruct~\cite{qwen2025qwen3} through a lightweight
modality projector; the LLM prompt accepts a hotword list or a
$3$--$5$\,s target-speaker reference at inference time, and a
dedicated noise-robustness SFT stage extends acoustic augmentation
to the plain-ASR branch.

On public benchmarks AmphionASR reaches $\mathbf{1.51}$\,\% CER on
AIShell-1 and $\mathbf{1.55}$\,\% WER on LibriSpeech
\texttt{test-clean}; with five in-prompt hotwords the
CommonVoice~17 Mandarin hotword CER drops from $5.93$\,\% to
$\mathbf{2.82}$\,\%, and target-speaker recognition reaches
$\mathbf{12.56}$\,\% WER on our internal \texttt{ts\_hw\_test}. On the
$5{,}000$-clip Voices-in-the-Wild-Bench~\cite{xie2026mega}
noise-robustness suite, AmphionASR leads $5$ of $16$ subsets
against eight publicly reported systems. Model weights and a
batched vLLM~\cite{kwon2023vllm} serving stack will be released at
\url{https://github.com/amphionspace/AmphionASR}; the training
code is not part of this release, and current gaps on LibriMix and
on the Sim-Echo and Sim-Recording cells of
Voices-in-the-Wild-Bench are documented in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.
