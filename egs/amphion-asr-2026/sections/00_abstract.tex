We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, an open-source industrial-grade SpeechLLM for Mandarin and English speech recognition, designed to address the failure modes that dominate real-world deployments: rare named entities, overlapping speakers, and real-world acoustic degradations.
The $4.7$\,B-parameter model provides a unified prompt-based interface that handles hotword lists, target-speaker reference audio, and language conditioning at inference time without any architectural modification between tasks, and is trained through a three-stage SFT recipe followed by a GRPO step with combined ASR-accuracy and hotword match-accuracy rewards; a dedicated degradation-robustness SFT stage on Voice-in-the-Wild-2M~\cite{xie2026mega} further covers real-world acoustic conditions.
On public benchmarks, AmphionASR achieves $\mathbf{1.51}$\,\% CER on AIShell-1 and $\mathbf{1.55}$\,\% WER on LibriSpeech \texttt{test-clean}. Its defining strengths are three deployment capabilities: (i)~\textbf{hotword conditioning}---five in-prompt hotwords halve Mandarin hotword CER ($5.93$\,\%$\,{\to}\,$$\mathbf{2.82}$\,\%), with English WER dropping from $7.79$\,\% to $\mathbf{5.01}$\,\%; (ii)~\textbf{target-speaker recognition}---$\mathbf{12.56}$\,\% WER on our internal \texttt{ts\_hw\_test}, roughly $4.2\times$ below the strongest open-source dual-audio baseline (Qwen3-Omni, $52.90$\,\%); and (iii)~\textbf{degradation robustness}---leading $5$ of $16$ subsets on the $5{,}000$-clip Voices-in-the-Wild-Bench against eight publicly reported systems, including closed-source models.
