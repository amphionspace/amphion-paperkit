We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, an open-source industrial-grade SpeechLLM for Mandarin and English speech recognition, designed to address the failure modes that dominate real-world deployments: rare named entities, overlapping speakers, and real-world acoustic degradations. The model couples a 24-layer Whisper-style audio encoder (${\approx}314$\,M parameters, initialised from the Qwen3-ASR audio tower) with Qwen3-4B-Instruct through a lightweight modality projector, totalling $4.7$\,B parameters. A unified prompt-based interface handles hotword lists and target-speaker reference audio at inference time, while a dedicated degradation-robustness SFT stage on Voice-in-the-Wild-2M~\cite{xie2026mega} covers real-world acoustic degradations for the plain-ASR branch.
On public benchmarks, AmphionASR achieves $\mathbf{1.51}$\,\% CER on AIShell-1 and $\mathbf{1.55}$\,\% WER on LibriSpeech \texttt{test-clean}. With five in-prompt hotwords, CommonVoice~17 Mandarin hotword CER drops from $5.93$\,\% to $\mathbf{2.82}$\,\%. Target-speaker recognition reaches $\mathbf{12.56}$\,\% WER on our internal \texttt{ts\_hw\_test}, roughly $4.2\times$ below the strongest open-source baseline (Qwen3-Omni, $52.90$\,\%). On the $5{,}000$-clip Voices-in-the-Wild-Bench degradation-robustness suite, AmphionASR leads $5$ of $16$ subsets against eight publicly reported systems.
