\section{Target-Speaker Recognition}
\label{sec:tsasr}

\subsection{Setup}

We evaluate target-speaker recognition (TS-ASR) on three test sets:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Internal hot+TS test set.} Generated by the same
        offline TS-ASR synthesiser as the training cuts
        (Section~\ref{subsec:tsasr-synth}) but with a held-out test
        configuration: a different random seed guarantees zero overlap
        with training, the total budget is capped at $10{,}000$ cuts,
        the per-speaker quota is widened to $40$ to broaden speaker
        coverage, and RIR augmentation is turned off so that
        reverberation is no longer a confound. The source pool covers
        the test splits of all ten Mandarin and four English ASR
        corpora used in training-time synthesis. Five percent of cuts
        are anti-hallucination negatives.
  \item \textbf{LibriMix~2 (Libri2Mix).} The standard public
        two-speaker mixture benchmark derived from LibriSpeech
        \cite{cosentino2020librimix}; we use the
        \texttt{test/16k/min/mix\_both} split ($6{,}000$ cuts).
  \item \textbf{LibriMix~3 (Libri3Mix).} Same release, three-speaker
        mixtures ($9{,}000$ cuts).
\end{itemize}

The prompt template is the TS-ASR entry of Table~\ref{tab:tasks}: a
$3$--$5$\,s enrollment audio segment is supplied as the first audio
content item, the mixed audio as the second, and the instruction
``\textit{Given the speaker's voice} \{\textit{enroll}\}
\textit{transcribe what this speaker says in the following audio.}''
ties them together. We report MER on the internal set (mixed
Mandarin--English vocabulary) and WER on LibriMix; for the negative
sub-slice we additionally report silence recall (the empty-hypothesis
rate) to verify that anti-hallucination training is taking effect.

\subsection{Analysis}

\paragraph{Strong on the in-domain set.}
On the internal hot+TS set, where the mixing protocol and the
reference-utterance pipeline match the training synthesiser, the
model reaches $13.06$\,\% MER.

\paragraph{Substantial gap on LibriMix.}
LibriMix numbers are much weaker -- $66.14$\,\% WER on Libri2Mix
and $86.71$\,\% on Libri3Mix. The training synthesiser already
includes LibriSpeech speakers (Table~\ref{tab:tsasr_sources}), so
this is not a pure speaker-coverage issue; the underlying cause
has not yet been diagnosed. We treat the gap as a known weakness
of the release and call it out explicitly in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations}; a controlled diagnostic is on the
agenda for the next training iteration.

\paragraph{Hotwords + target speaker (combined).}
The released checkpoint supports hotword and target-speaker
prompting simultaneously: in the TS-ASR template
(Table~\ref{tab:tasks}), a hotword line is appended after the
speaker instruction, so the model jointly conditions on the
enrollment audio and the in-prompt biasing list. A dedicated combined
benchmark (TS-ASR with $k$ hotwords supplied) is in preparation and
will appear in a future revision.

\paragraph{Anti-hallucination.}
On the negative sub-slice of the internal hot+TS test set, the model
achieves a silence recall above $90$\,\%
