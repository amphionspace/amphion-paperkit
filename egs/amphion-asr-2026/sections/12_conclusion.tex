\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{AmphionASR}, an open-source, industrial-grade
SpeechLLM for personalised Mandarin and English speech recognition.
The model couples a Whisper-style 24-layer audio encoder
($\approx\!314$\,M parameters, initialised from the Qwen3-ASR audio
tower) to the Qwen3-4B-Instruct language model through a
lightweight modality projector and four learnable modality-boundary
prompts -- $\approx\!4.7$\,B parameters in total ($\approx\!4.3$\,B
distinct after input--output embedding tying) -- and is trained in
three stages: audio--text alignment, continue-SFT for Mandarin and
English transcription, and capability SFT that adds in-prompt
hotword conditioning and reference-utterance target-speaker
recognition. On Mandarin benchmarks the v3 checkpoint reaches
single-digit CER on every reported set, with AISHELL-1 at
$1.85$\,\% as the headline data point. On English it covers the
standard LibriSpeech, GigaSpeech, MLS, CommonVoice and FLEURS
grid, with LibriSpeech \texttt{test-clean} at $1.82$\,\% WER. On
TALCS Mandarin--English code-switching it reaches $7.48$\,\% MER.
With as few as five in-prompt hotwords on the CommonVoice~17
hotword splits, CER and WER drop by roughly half (oracle prompt).
A parameter-free text-space retrieval operator
(Section~\ref{subsec:retrieve-algorithm}) extends this prompt-driven
hotword behaviour to deployment-scale pools by reducing the
prompt to a per-utterance top-$K$ subset of a pool of size up to
$10^4$. A noise-robustness SFT variant (v3-NR;
Section~\ref{subsec:v3nr}) is the first AmphionASR checkpoint with
a controlled noise-robustness evaluation alongside the standard
benchmarks.

What still needs to be solved -- and is the explicit subject of
the next release -- is closing the gap on the public multi-talker
LibriMix benchmarks, narrowing the residual gap to
Qwen3-ASR-1.7B~\cite{qwen2025qwen3asr} on the non-MUSAN-speech
rows of Table~\ref{tab:noise_robustness}, documenting the v3-NR
training pipeline alongside the v3 SFT recipe, and upgrading the
inference path with a measured streaming mode. We will release
model weights and the vLLM-based serving stack at
\url{https://github.com/amphionspace/AmphionASR}; the training
pipeline is documented in
Sections~\ref{sec:data}--\ref{sec:training} but the training code
itself is not part of this release
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).
