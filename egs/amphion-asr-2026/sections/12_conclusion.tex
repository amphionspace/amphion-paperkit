\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{AmphionASR}, an open-source, industrial-grade
SpeechLLM for personalised Mandarin and English speech recognition.
The model couples the Qwen3-AuT audio tower (24-layer Whisper-style
encoder, $d{=}1024$, 314\,M parameters) to the
Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 language model through a 9.2\,M-parameter
Linear--SwooshR--Linear projector and four learnable
modality-boundary prompt vectors -- $4.7$\,B parameters in total
($4.3$\,B distinct after \texttt{lm\_head} tying) -- and is trained
in three stages: audio--text alignment, continue-SFT for Mandarin
and English transcription, and capability SFT that adds in-prompt
hotword conditioning and reference-utterance target-speaker
recognition. On Mandarin benchmarks the v3 checkpoint
reaches single-digit CER on most sets, including AISHELL-1 at
$1.80$\%; on English it covers the standard Librispeech / GigaSpeech /
MLS / CommonVoice grid; on TALCS Mandarin--English code-switching it
reaches $14.99$\% MER; and with as few as five in-prompt hotwords the
CommonVoice CER and WER drop by roughly half.

What still needs to be solved -- and is the explicit subject of the
next release -- is closing the gap on the public multi-talker LibriMix
benchmarks, training a noise-robust variant that competes with
Qwen3-ASR-1.7B on far-field / reverberant audio, and upgrading the
inference path with a measured streaming mode. We will release model
weights, inference code, and the eval scripts that produced every
number in this report at
