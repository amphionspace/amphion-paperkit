\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\model uses one model across diverse acoustic and conversational scenarios, combining enrollment-conditioned encoder attention with training that covers target absence in each scene type.
\bench evaluates this coverage through paired conditions and real recordings.
Under the matched protocol, \model reduces both target-present error rates and target-absent false alarms against CocktailASR-1, in Mandarin and in an English counterpart, and its error and false-alarm rates are much lower than those of two extract-then-recognize cascades, although one cascade completely misses fewer target utterances.
Short similar-sounding voices, false alarms in English real conversations, and the small English real section remain limitations.
Testing both target presence and absence is essential to assess recognition across scenarios.
