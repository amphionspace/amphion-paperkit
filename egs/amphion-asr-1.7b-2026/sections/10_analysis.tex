\section{Cross-Task Observations}
\label{sec:analysis}

\subsection{Variation across General-ASR Conditions}

AmphionASR's highest general-ASR errors occur on GigaSpeech and the
WenetSpeech meeting split (Tables~\ref{tab:asr_en}--\ref{tab:asr_zh}).
Both contain more spontaneous speech than the read-speech sets.

The current evaluation does not separate acoustic, linguistic, and
segmentation errors within these conditions.
We therefore report the variation without assigning a mechanism.

\subsection{Variation across Target-Speaker Mixtures}

Target-speaker WER differs substantially between the in-house test set
and the public Libri2Mix and Libri3Mix tests
(Table~\ref{tab:tsasr}).
The gap is the main unresolved cross-condition result in this report.

The available experiments do not isolate corpus, synthesis, or
interferer-count effects.
Explaining the gap requires controlled comparisons beyond the current
evaluation.
