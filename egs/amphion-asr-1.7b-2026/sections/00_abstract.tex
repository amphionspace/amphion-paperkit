We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, an industrial-grade SpeechLLM for Mandarin and English speech recognition, built on the Qwen3-ASR-1.7B audio encoder and language backbone.
The model handles hotword lists and target-speaker reference audio with no architectural changes between tasks, and is trained through a multi-source LoRA SFT recipe that mixes degradation-robustness, hotword, target-speaker, and whispered-speech data.
On public benchmarks, AmphionASR reaches $\mathbf{1.34}$\,\% CER on AIShell-1 and $\mathbf{1.59}$\,\% WER on LibriSpeech \texttt{test-clean}.
Its defining strengths are four deployment capabilities: (i)~\textbf{hotword conditioning with retrieval-based contextual biasing}---a dual-encoder neural retriever operating on frozen acoustic and text embeddings scales in-prompt contextual biasing, attaining Recall@$50$ above $94$\,\% on CommonVoice and competitive biased-error rates on GigaSpeechBench vertical domains~\cite{tu2026gigaspeechbench}; (ii)~\textbf{target-speaker recognition}---$\mathbf{13.02}$\,\% WER on the positive sub-slice of our in-house target-speaker test set, roughly $4.1\times$ below the strongest open-source dual-audio baseline (Qwen3-Omni, $52.90$\,\%); (iii)~\textbf{degradation robustness}---competitive results on Voice-in-the-Wild-Bench against eight publicly reported systems; and (iv)~\textbf{whispered-speech ASR}---whisper-specialised SFT on the WhispEar corpus~\cite{fang2025whisperear} enables low-WER transcription on Mandarin whisper test sets.
