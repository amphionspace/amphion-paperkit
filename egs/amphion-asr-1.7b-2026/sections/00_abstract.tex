Practical speech recognition often requires more than unconditioned transcription: users may provide domain vocabulary or reference speech, while the input may contain degradations or whispered speech.
We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, a SpeechLLM built on Qwen3-ASR-1.7B
that supports these conditions through a shared model and
task-specific inputs.
We evaluate the system on Mandarin and English benchmarks.
Its integrated large-vocabulary hotword-conditioning pipeline attains Recall@$50$
above $94$\,\% on CommonVoice and reduces entity error on
GigaSpeechBench vertical domains~\cite{tu2026gigaspeechbench}, while the
same shared model also serves target-speaker ASR, degradation-robust
ASR, and whispered-speech ASR within a unified study of four
recognition settings.
Plain transcription is retained as a retention check across multiple standard ASR
benchmarks rather than as a state-of-the-art claim.
