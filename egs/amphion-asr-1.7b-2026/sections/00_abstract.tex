Practical speech recognition often requires more than unconditioned transcription: users may provide domain vocabulary or reference speech, while the input may contain degradations or whispered speech.
We present \textbf{AmphionASR}, a Mandarin--English SpeechLLM built on Qwen3-ASR-1.7B that supports these conditions through a shared model and task-specific inputs.
The system combines a three-stage LoRA SFT recipe with a dual-encoder retriever that selects a compact hotword subset from a larger candidate vocabulary.
On public ASR benchmarks, AmphionASR reaches $\mathbf{1.34}$\,\% CER on AIShell-1 and $\mathbf{1.59}$\,\% WER on LibriSpeech \texttt{test-clean}.
For contextual ASR, the retriever attains Recall@$50$ above $94$\,\% on CommonVoice and reduces biased error on GigaSpeechBench vertical domains~\cite{tu2026gigaspeechbench}.
The same model is evaluated for target-speaker ASR, degradation-robust ASR, and whispered-speech ASR, providing a unified study of four recognition settings.
