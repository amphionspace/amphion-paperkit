\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{AmphionASR}, a Mandarin--English SpeechLLM that
uses a shared model for general, hotword-conditioned, target-speaker,
degradation-robust, and whispered-speech ASR.
The system combines staged fine-tuning with optional task conditions
and retrieval from larger hotword vocabularies.

The evaluation shows that hotword retrieval provides the clearest
improvement over the corresponding unconditioned setting, while the
plain-ASR results characterise retention of the backbone's general
recognition quality.
Target-speaker transfer across mixture conditions and unmeasured
generation failures remain the main limits of the current evidence.
