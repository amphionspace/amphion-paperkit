\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{AmphionASR}, a SpeechLLM built on Qwen3-ASR-1.7B that
uses a shared model for general, hotword-conditioned, target-speaker,
degradation-robust, and whispered-speech ASR.
The system combines staged fine-tuning with optional task conditions
and retrieval from larger hotword vocabularies.
The current evaluation covers Mandarin and English benchmarks.

The complete retrieval-conditioned hotword pipeline provides the clearest
observed improvement over the no-hotword-context setting, while the
plain-ASR results characterise retention of the backbone's general
recognition quality.
This comparison does not isolate the retriever's contribution.
The target-speaker evidence covers only positive slices, leaving
target-absent behaviour and transfer across mixture conditions outside
the conclusions supported by the current experiments.
