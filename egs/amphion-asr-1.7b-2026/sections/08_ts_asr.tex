\paragraph{Strong positive-WER improvement over the open baselines.}
On the positive sub-slice of our in-house target-speaker test set,
AmphionASR reaches $13.02$\,\% WER, roughly $4.1\times$ below the
strongest open-source dual-audio baseline (Qwen3-Omni, $52.90$\,\%) and
about $6.5\times$ below Qwen3-ASR-0.6B. The improvement is consistent
on the public LibriMix tests: $63.04$\,\% on Libri2Mix ($2.2\times$
below Qwen3-Omni's $135.91$\,\%) and $85.04$\,\% on Libri3Mix
($1.6\times$ below Qwen3-Omni's $138.41$\,\%). The LibriMix WER values
exceed $100$\,\% for the three open baselines because their hypothesis
on mixed-both audio contains the transcripts of all speakers
concatenated together (the WER is
$\text{insertions}/|\text{ref}| \approx 100$--$200$\,\% when the
reference is the target speaker only); none of the three open systems
exhibits any speaker selection. AmphionASR brings these two columns
under $100$\,\%, but with a larger Libri2Mix $\to$ Libri3Mix
degradation ($+22.00$ WER points) than the open baselines (at most
$5.65$ points, Qwen3-ASR-1.7B), indicating that speaker selection
degrades more steeply as the interferer count grows.
