\subsection{Whispered-Speech ASR}
\label{sec:whisper-asr}

We evaluate whispered-speech recognition on two test sets: the
Mandarin wEar split and the English WTIMIT
split~\cite{fang2025whisperear}. CER is reported for Mandarin and WER
for English. On the Mandarin wEar test set AmphionASR reaches
$0.58$\,\% CER, below Qwen3-ASR-1.7B's $1.09$\,\%; on the English
WTIMIT split it reaches $6.11$\,\% WER, below Qwen3-ASR-1.7B's
$10.30$\,\%.
