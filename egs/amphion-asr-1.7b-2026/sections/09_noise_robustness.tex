Table~\ref{tab:vitw_bench} reports the sixteen-subset breakdown.
We compare AmphionASR against eight publicly reported systems:
Gemini~3-Flash~\cite{comanici2025gemini}, Seed-ASR~\cite{bai2024seedasr},
GPT-4o-transcribe~\cite{openai2024gpt4o},
Whisper-large-v3~\cite{radford2023whisper},
Qwen2.5-Omni~\cite{xu2025qwen25omni}, Kimi-Audio~\cite{kimiaudio2025},
Qwen3-ASR-1.7B~\cite{qwen2025qwen3asr}, and Mega-ASR~\cite{xie2026mega}
(with its environment-aware routing variant).

\paragraph{Subset-level results.}
On the \emph{Real} split AmphionASR achieves the lowest WER on
Obstructed ($1.38$\,\%), Echo \& reverberation ($7.91$\,\%),
Recording coloration ($6.32$\,\%), and Transmission dropout
($2.32$\,\%). On the \emph{Sim} split it leads on Noise ($7.24$\,\%),
Echo \& reverberation ($11.49$\,\%), Recording coloration
($11.55$\,\%), and Mixed ($4.19$\,\%). The remaining rows are led
primarily by Mega-ASR or Qwen3-ASR-1.7B.

\paragraph{Mixed subsets.}
On the Mixed subsets AmphionASR reaches $2.85$\,\% WER on Real-Mixed,
second to Mega-ASR's $2.63$\,\%, and $4.19$\,\% WER on Sim-Mixed, the
lowest in the column (below Mega-ASR's $4.53$\,\%).

\paragraph{Echo and recording coloration.}
AmphionASR leads both Sim-Echo \& reverberation ($11.49$\,\% vs.\
Mega-ASR's $12.22$\,\%) and Sim-Recording coloration ($11.55$\,\%
vs.\ Mega-ASR's $13.23$\,\%); these remain the highest-WER Sim rows
for AmphionASR in absolute terms.
